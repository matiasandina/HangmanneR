About HangmanneR

I wanted to make something fun for the TidyTuesday Challenge, so I decided to give a try to coding a Hangman App. It took a while for me to actually sit down and write HangmanneR, but the coding itself was super fun! I hope two things. First, I hope you enjoy playing it. Second, I hope it gets you curious and helps you learn about a package you didn't know about!
